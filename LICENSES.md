## The file that cannot be licensed

`data/recommendations.json` holds 773 bulleted recommendations **in the
document's own words**. Those words are

> © 2023 by The Heritage Foundation. All rights reserved.

Nobody but the rightsholder can grant permission to reuse them, and this
repository does not pretend otherwise. They are reproduced here as quotation for
the purpose of documentation and comment: each entry is a single sentence,
attributed, anchored to a printed page, and assembled into a finding aid that
serves a different purpose from the original. That is a considered position, not
a licence, and it binds nobody else. If you want to reuse these sentences in
your own work, the question of permission is between you and the rightsholder.

The volume's publisher distributes it as a free download. Free to download is
not free to redistribute, which is why **no running text is in this repository**
and why the concordance works on a copy the reader fetches themselves.

## Why the factual data is CC0

Chapter boundaries, the offset between PDF page and printed page, counts, and
the table of which stage rests on which URL are compilations of fact. They were
laborious to establish and are not works of authorship. A tracker whose evidence
table others cannot reuse and check would defeat its own purpose, so they are
released without conditions.
