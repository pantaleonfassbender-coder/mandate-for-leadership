What this site does and does not do. It documents two things: what the document proposes, with a page citation, and what the public record shows, with a primary source. It offers no assessment of either. Correspondence between a proposal and a government action is not evidence that the document caused it, and the tracker measures correspondence only — not the administration's record as a whole.
